Before you join

Your camera and audio will be shown live to strangers on a random video chat site. You must be 18+.

TrioMeet collects no analytics and has no accounts. Video and audio travel directly between you and the host, or through an encrypted TURN relay if a direct connection cannot be made. Only the PeerJS signaling service sees connection setup data.

You can leave at any time with the Leave button. Closing this page also stops your camera and microphone.
